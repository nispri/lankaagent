## Demo Flow (15 min)

### 1. "This Is My Site" (2 min)
> **Action:** Show ceyloria-site.vercel.app homepage
> **Say:** "I'm Nishantha. I run Ceyloria Holidays — SLTDA licensed inbound operator. This widget on the bottom-right? I built it because *I needed it*."

### 2. Live Chat — English (3 min)
> **Action:** Click widget → type: *"Hi, we're 2 couples from Germany, want 10 days culture + beach in March"*
> **Watch:** AI responds with qualification questions
> **Say:** "Notice: asks pax, dates, budget — structured lead capture. No 'just browsing'."

### 3. Multilingual Switch (2 min)
> **Action:** Type in German: *"Hallo, ich möchte nach Sri Lanka reisen"*
> **Watch:** AI responds in fluent German
> **Say:** "7 languages native. Russian, German, French, Chinese, Sinhala, Tamil. No Google Translate layer — model speaks them."

### 4. Lead → Itinerary (4 min)
> **Action:** Complete lead flow → name, email, dates, pax, budget
> **Watch:** "Generating your itinerary..." → day-by-day output appears
> **Say:** "10 seconds. Sellable quote. Your agent would take 30 min. This runs 24/7."

### 5. Wellness Upsell (2 min)
> **Action:** Type: *"Add Ayurveda to this trip"*
> **Watch:** Wellness protocol appears with treatments, pricing, doctor assignment
> **Say:** "$199 wellness upsell. You keep 15% = $30 per booking. Pure margin."

### 6. Dashboard Proof (2 min)
> **Action:** Open `/api/v1/leads` tab → show the lead you just created
> **Say:** "Your leads. Your data. Row-level security — nobody else sees it. Export anytime."
